# Implementation Plan: Dashboard Header UI Refinement

## 1. Objective
Streamline the League Dashboard header by removing the "This Week's Bet" (Coupon Stake) financial card. The goal is to focus user attention solely on the "Saved Pot" while ensuring the layout remains balanced, responsive, and visually "premium."

## 2. Targeted Component
- **File:** `pages/Dashboard.tsx`
- **Section:** The financial status container located within the main League Header Card (approx. lines 90-110).

## 3. Structural Modifications
- **Step 1: Removal of the Indigo Card**
    - Identify and delete the JSX block for the "Coupon Stake Card" (the one using `from-indigo-900 to-indigo-800` and the `Banknote` icon).
- **Step 2: Adjusting the Flex Container**
    - The parent div of the financial cards currently uses `flex flex-col sm:flex-row gap-4`. 
    - Since only one card remains, the `gap-4` will no longer be necessary but harmless. 
    - The `flex-1` property on the remaining "Savings Pot Card" might cause it to stretch fully across its container on mobile.
- **Step 3: Visual Balancing & Width Control**
    - To prevent the single card from looking "stretched" on wide screens, a `max-w-xs` (max-width: 20rem) or similar constraint will be applied to the "Savings Pot Card".
    - On desktop (`lg` breakpoint), ensure the card remains right-aligned to balance the League Name and stats which are left-aligned.

## 4. Aesthetic & Spacing Refinements
- **Consistency:** Ensure the vertical alignment of the "Saved Pot" card matches the optical center of the text content to its left.
- **Cleanliness:** Remove unused variables or calculations related to `thisWeekCouponTotal` and `couponStakePerPerson` to keep the code performant and clean.
- **Mobile Fluidity:** On mobile, the card should stack naturally below the header text with consistent padding (`mt-5`). On desktop, the `lg:mt-0` will ensure a seamless horizontal layout.

## 5. Success Criteria (Verification)
- **Visual:** The header looks intentional and "complete," not as if a piece is missing.
- **Responsiveness:**
    - Mobile: The "Saved Pot" card is centered or full-width with proper padding.
    - Desktop: The card sits elegantly on the right, providing a clear financial summary at a glance.
- **Stability:** No regressions in the surrounding layout (Leaderboard, Pick Form, etc.).
