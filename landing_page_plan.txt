# Landing Page Design Plan: Coupon Busters

## 1. Branding & Visual Identity
- **Primary Colors:** Emerald Green (`bg-emerald-800`), Gold (`text-yellow-400` for "Banker" accents), Slate (`text-slate-900` for text).
- **Vibe:** Professional, exciting, trustworthy (financial context), and communal (team spirit).
- **Typography:** Inter (Clean, modern sans-serif).

## 2. Page Structure & Content

### A. Navigation Bar
- **Logo:** "Coupon Busters" with Trophy Icon.
- **Links:** How it Works, Benefits, Pricing, FAQ.
- **Actions:** "Login" (Secondary), "Start a League" (Primary CTA - Emerald Button).

### B. Hero Section
- **Headline:** "The Football Betting League That Builds a Pot."
- **Subheadline:** "Run your work or pub syndicate without the headache. We automate the weekly collections, the picks, and the payouts. You just pick the winners."
- **Primary CTA:** "Create Your League"
- **Secondary CTA:** "See Demo"
- **Visual:** A split-screen graphic showing a winning "Coupon" slip on one side and a growing "Pot of Gold" on the other.

### C. Social Proof (Trust Signals)
- **Stats Bar:** 
  - "£50,000+ Paid Out This Season"
  - "1,200+ Active Leagues"
  - "100% Secure Payments (Stripe)"
- **Logos:** "As seen in" or "Technology partners" (Stripe, SportRadar icons).

### D. Use Cases (Who is this for?)
- **The Office Syndicate:** "Stop chasing Dave from Accounts for his £2. We handle the payments automatically."
- **The Pub Team:** "Raise money for the end-of-season tour while having a punt every week."
- **Mates Groups:** "Settle the debate on who knows ball, and build a holiday fund while you do it."

### E. Pain Points (The "Before" State)
- **Problem 1:** "Chasing payments every week is awkward and time-consuming."
- **Problem 2:** "Keeping track of who picked who on WhatsApp is a nightmare."
- **Problem 3:** "One bad bet and the money is gone with nothing to show for it."

### F. Why Us? (The Solution)
- **Automated Wallet:** Users deposit funds; we auto-deduct weekly fees. No pay, no play.
- **The Banker System:** Distinct "Banker" (Real Money) vs "Cover" (Points) system adds strategy.
- **The Safety Pot:** Even if the bet loses, a portion of the entry fee goes into a locked "Season Pot" for a guaranteed payout/party at the end.

### G. How It Works (4 Steps)
1.  **Join a League:** Request access and pick your Representative Team (e.g., Swansea City).
2.  **Weekly Picks:** Choose your "Banker" (goes on the real bet) and your "Cover" (for points).
3.  **The Accumulator:** The Admin gets a generated "Coupon" of all Banker picks to place the bet.
4.  **Win or Save:** If the bet wins, instant payout. If it loses, the Pot grows.

### H. Benefits
- **Transparent Admin:** Everyone sees who paid and who picked.
- **Secure Handling:** Money is held securely until payout or pot withdrawal.
- **Leaderboards:** Real-time scoring and league tables.

### I. Pricing & Money Split (The Model)
