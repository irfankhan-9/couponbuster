# Implementation Plan: Dashboard UI Refinement

## 1. Objective
Refine the `Dashboard.tsx` header by removing the "This Week's Bet" financial card while maintaining a balanced, professional, and visually appealing layout. The "Saved Pot" card will remain as the primary financial indicator.

## 2. Targeted Component
- **File:** `pages/Dashboard.tsx`
- **Location:** Inside the `<Layout>` component, within the "League Header Card" container.

## 3. Structural Changes
- **Removal:** Delete the JSX block representing the "Coupon Stake Card" (the indigo gradient card featuring the `Banknote` icon).
- **Layout Adjustment:** 
    - The container currently uses `flex flex-col sm:flex-row gap-4` and `lg:ml-4`.
    - After removal, the remaining "Savings Pot Card" (emerald gradient) will be the sole element in this flex container.
    - To maintain visual balance, the "Savings Pot Card" should either:
        a) Retain its `flex-1` property but be given a maximum width constraint to prevent excessive stretching on large screens.
        b) Be converted to a fixed-width container that aligns elegantly with the right-hand side of the header on desktop.

## 4. Visual & Aesthetic Refinements
- **Consistency:** Ensure the vertical alignment of the "Saved Pot" card matches the baseline of the league title and stats on the left.
- **Spacing:** Verify that the `lg:mt-0` and `mt-5` margins provide adequate breathing room between the league description and the single financial card on mobile and tablet views.
- **Emphasis:** Since "Saved Pot" is now the singular focus, the card's elevation (shadow) and gradient intensity will be preserved to maintain its status as a "key metric" for the user.

## 5. Verification Steps
- Check mobile view (375px) to ensure the card stacks correctly below the header text.
- Check tablet view (768px) to ensure the `sm:flex-row` transition handles the single card without awkward left-justification.
- Check desktop view (1280px+) to ensure the card remains anchored to the right, balancing the "League Name" section on the left.
