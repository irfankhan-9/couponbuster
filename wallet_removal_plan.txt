# Implementation Plan: Global Wallet & Fund Section Removal

## 1. Objective
Systematically remove all "Wallet" displays, "Deposit Funds" calls-to-action, and financial balance indicators from the user interface. The goal is to transition the app from an automated payment platform to a pure coordination/coaching tool for syndicates, where financial transactions happen externally.

## 2. Targeted Components & Files

### A. Global Navigation (`components/Layout.tsx`)
- **Desktop Nav:** Remove the `flex` container featuring the "Wallet:" label and `formatCurrency(currentUser?.wallet_balance_pence)`.
- **Mobile Nav:** Remove the `ml-auto` badge showing the wallet balance in the user profile section of the mobile drawer.
- **Iconography:** Remove any references to "Wallet" or "Banknote" icons within the navigation context.

### B. League Dashboard (`pages/Dashboard.tsx`)
- **Sidebar Actions:** Remove the entire `<div className="bg-emerald-50 ...">` block titled "Deposit Funds."
- **Visual Balance:** With the "Deposit Funds" section gone, the "Leaderboard" will become the primary sidebar element. Ensure the vertical spacing between the Leaderboard and the bottom of the page remains consistent.

### C. Admin Panel (`pages/Admin.tsx`)
- **Payout Tooling:** Update the "Coupon Generator" tool. While the "Payout Share" calculator is useful, ensure the text does not imply the app is physically moving money. Change labels from "Payout Share" to "Suggested Payout per Person."
- **Dashboard Cards:** Ensure no individual member wallet balances are displayed in the points management or member list sections.

### D. Landing Page (`pages/Landing.tsx`)
- **Hero/Benefits:** 
    - Remove the "Automated Wallet" feature card under "Professional Grade League Management."
    - Replace or re-word "Secure Payments via Stripe" trust signal to focus on "Secure Data & League Management."
- **Pricing Section:** Refactor the "Where does the money go?" section to focus on the *concept* of the split (Syndicate Stake vs Pot) rather than the "Automated Collection" mechanism.
- **FAQ:** Remove questions related to "How do I withdraw funds?" and "Automated collections." Replace with "How do we handle the money?" (Answer: External to the platform).

## 3. Structural & Aesthetic Adjustments
- **Layout Reflow:** On the Dashboard, the removal of the sidebar card will create significant white space. The "Leaderboard" should potentially be given more vertical prominence or the "Recent Activity" list (if restored) should balance the left side.
- **Code Cleanup:** Once the UI is removed, identify unused imports such as `Wallet` from `lucide-react` and `formatCurrency` in files where it was only used for balance displays.

## 4. Verification Steps
- **Mobile Check:** Ensure the mobile menu looks clean without the empty space where the balance badge used to be.
- **User Flow:** Verify that a new user can join and participate in a league without ever seeing a "0.00" balance or being prompted to deposit money.
- **Landing Page Consistency:** Ensure no "Stripe" or "Payment" logos remain if the feature is fully removed to avoid user confusion.
